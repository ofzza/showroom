# _`P10.5`_ The console

implemented 2026-11-14, re-approved with _`D436`_, _`D437`_ (option chosen: "Approve and commit"); approved 2026-11-14 (option chosen: "Approve and commit (Rec.)"), after its zero-context review; _`D436`_, _`D437`_ added at its browser check (2026-11-14), for re-approval at its checkpoint; edited 2026-11-14 after its zero-context review (`./.workflow/.temp/review-p10.5/`: 20 findings; QA–QD settled as _`D432`_–_`D435`_, 15 fixes to the steps and facts, 1 later (_`L14`_), 1 skip)

## Goals

_`D369`_ and _`D377`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md): the `ts` and `react` showcase adapters' console panel no longer grows the showcase frame with each line: on its own at a fixed height while the showcase renders nothing; once it renders, hidden behind fading lines and a counter that opens it over the showcase; errors kept noticeable; the switch made at run time, for good. Takes _`O60`_ (the console's details) and _`O65`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md) for this segment.

Not in _`P10.5`_: the showroom (`src/app/`), the code pane and the frame script's contract, none of which changes; the `html` showcase adapter, which has no console panel.

Facts found while planning (2026-11-14):

- The console script (`consoleScript()`, `src/adapters/showcase/ts/console/`) is self-contained, inlined as its source text into every `ts` and `react` showcase frame page, after the showcase frame root (`<div id="root">`) and the console panel's element (`<div id="showroom-console" hidden>`), and before the showcase's module. The panel lives in the element's shadow root, styled with `light-dark()` from the `color-scheme` the frame script sets on `<html>`.
- Today the panel is hidden until its first line, then grows with each line up to `CONSOLE_PANEL_MAX_LINES` (12, 18 px each), scrolling beyond them, and dispatches `CONSOLE_GROWTH_EVENT` with each growth so the frame script's guard (`MAX_REPEATED_GROWTHS`, `src/frame/`) doesn't count it as a repeated growth.
- The frame script measures `<body>`'s bottom and its children's bottoms (with their margins), each by `getBoundingClientRect()`: a child of `<body>` with `position: fixed` at the bottom measures at the viewport's bottom, so the showcase frame could never shrink below it; fixed elements inside a child's shadow root aren't measured, as they don't change the child's own box. A `min-height` on `<body>` is measured.
- The module script runs before the window's `load` event; React's first commit (`createRoot().render()`) may come just after it.
- The code pane's minimum is 6 lines (`CODE_PANE_MIN_LINES`, `src/app/code-pane/`; _`D405`_, _`D416`_ (PLAN-P10.4-code-pane.md)); at its full state the showcase frame is `inert`, so the counter can't be clicked then.
- While the code pane is hidden, its cycling button floats over the showcase frame's top-right corner (_`D407`_ (PLAN-P10.4-code-pane.md)).
- Tests touching the console beyond its own: `src/adapters/showcase/ts/index.test.ts` (the page), the `ts` and `react` integration tests, `build()`'s `e2e` and the `serve` tier (the page's markup).

## Steps

1. **The console script** (`src/adapters/showcase/ts/console/`) (_`D369`_, _`D377`_, _`D424`_–_`D435`_): `CONSOLE_PANEL_MAX_LINES` becomes `CONSOLE_PANEL_LINES` (8), with new constants for the fading lines (`CONSOLE_FADING_LINES` 3, `CONSOLE_LINE_SHOWN_MS` 3000, `CONSOLE_LINE_FADE_MS` 1000), the grace period (`CONSOLE_GRACE_MS` 100) and the ceiling (`CONSOLE_MODE_CEILING_MS` 2000), all passed in `ConsoleScriptConstants`. Everything it uses comes from `window` (`window.setTimeout`, `clearTimeout`, `MutationObserver`, `getComputedStyle`, `document.body`), as it is self-contained. The script:
   - **the panel's element** (the shadow host, after the showcase frame root) stays in the page's flow in every mode; the page's markup keeps its `hidden`, removed on the first line, in every mode; in the hidden and open modes the element is 0 px high (no margin, padding or border), and only the elements inside its shadow root are `position: fixed` (`z-index: 2147483647`, the open panel opaque), so the frame script never measures them (see the facts);
   - **picking the mode** (_`D428`_, _`D435`_): keeps every line from the start (formatting unchanged), and picks its mode 100 ms after the window's `load` event, or 2 s after `DOMContentLoaded`, whichever comes first (a page already `complete` when the script starts counts as loaded); **alone** if nothing has rendered by then, **hidden** otherwise; a line logged earlier waits for the mode;
   - **rendered** (_`D430`_, changed by _`D433`_; _`D377`_): judged with a `MutationObserver` on `<body>` (child lists, subtrees, text), and by scanning `<body>` again when the mode is picked; sticky (content seen once counts, even if removed before the pick); a node inside an excluded element, or inside the panel's element, never counts; the observer is disconnected once the mode is hidden (picked, or switched to); without `window.MutationObserver`, only the scan at the pick decides;
   - **alone**: the panel at the fixed height of `CONSOLE_PANEL_LINES` lines from its first line on (_`D424`_), in the flow below the showcase frame root, scrolling to its newest line while scrolled to its bottom; `CONSOLE_GROWTH_EVENT` dispatched once, as it appears, and only by the amount the page actually grew; switching to hidden the first time content appears, never back (_`D369`_): the panel's element goes to 0 px (the showcase frame shrinks to its content), and the lines already logged only count in the counter (no fading lines replayed);
   - **hidden**: the `CONSOLE_FADING_LINES` newest lines at the bottom, left of the counter, translucent, ignoring the pointer, `aria-hidden`, each the first line of its text, cut with an ellipsis, faded after `CONSOLE_LINE_SHOWN_MS` over `CONSOLE_LINE_FADE_MS`, then removed (_`D425`_); a line pushed out by a newer one has its timers cleared; a showcase frame too short for 3 lines shows those that fit; the counter (_`D426`_): a `button` pill in the bottom-right corner, hidden while nothing has been logged, `>_ <count>` (one per call or uncaught error, not per text line), semi-transparent until hovered or focused; red, as `>_ <count> · <errors> error(s)` (`console.error`, uncaught errors, rejected promises), from the first error until the console is next opened (_`D427`_); its accessible name says the count and the errors (e.g. "Open the console: 12 lines, 2 errors"; singular for 1);
   - **open** (from hidden): the whole panel over the showcase from the bottom, a slim header bar (`Console · <count> line(s)` and a close button labelled "Close the console") above `CONSOLE_PANEL_LINES` lines (_`D424`_, _`D429`_), a `role="region"` labelled "Console", scrolled to its newest line, and following new lines only while scrolled to its bottom; the fading lines and the counter hidden while it is open; opened by the counter, moving focus to the close button; closed by the close button or Escape (a `keydown` listener on `window`, in the capture phase, acting only while open, otherwise leaving the event alone), returning focus to the counter if focus was inside the panel; opened by itself, never taking focus, by the first uncaught error or rejected promise after the showcase rendered, once per showcase frame load (_`D377`_, _`D432`_), and, when the mode is picked hidden, by such an error logged before the pick; while open over content shorter than it, `<body>` gets an inline `min-height` of the larger of its computed `min-height` and the open panel's height, its own inline value saved and restored on closing (so the showcase frame grows once, and shrinks back), the growth announced with `CONSOLE_GROWTH_EVENT` (_`D431`_);
   - **detached**: if the showcase replaces `<body>`'s content, detaching the panel's element (`isConnected` false), a line does nothing in the panel, and never throws; the browser's console still gets every call;
   - accepted: in a showcase frame taller than the screen, the fading lines, the counter and the open console sit at the bottom of the showcase frame, possibly out of view (_`D434`_);
   - its JSDoc and header comment updated; the frame script (`src/frame/`) unchanged, its JSDoc on `CONSOLE_GROWTH_EVENT` updated if it no longer fits.

## Decisions

- _`D424`_ (`live`, refined by _`D436`_; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); resolves _`O60`_'s number of lines; Q1, the user: "Same number of lines as min line count for showcase syntax viewers/tabs in 50:50 mode - I believe it is 8 lines", then, told the code pane's minimum is 6, option chosen: "8 lines, separate"): **The console panel's fixed height is 8 lines**, alone and open, its own constant (`CONSOLE_PANEL_LINES`), independent of the code pane's minimum. Rejected: 12 lines (today's maximum, the recommendation); growing with its lines up to 12 (reopens _`D369`_); sharing the code pane's minimum of 6, as one constant in `src/frame/` (the follow-up's recommendation).
- _`D425`_ (`live`; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); resolves _`O60`_'s lines showing and fading; Q2, option chosen: "3 lines, 3s + 1s fade (Rec.)"): **The 3 newest lines show at the bottom, each for 3 s, then fade over 1 s**, one line each, cut with an ellipsis. Rejected: only the newest line, 2 s and a 0.5 s fade (a burst flashes past unread); 5 lines, 4 s and a 1 s fade (covers more of a small showcase).
- _`D426`_ (`live`; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); resolves _`O60`_'s counter; Q3, option chosen: "Bottom-right pill (Rec.)"): **The counter is a small pill in the showcase frame's bottom-right corner**, `>_ <count>`, semi-transparent until hovered or focused, hidden while nothing has been logged; away from the code pane's cycling button in the top-right corner (_`D407`_ (PLAN-P10.4-code-pane.md)). Rejected: the bottom-left corner (where a showcase's content more likely sits); the top-left corner (apart from the fading lines).
- _`D427`_ (`live`; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); resolves _`O60`_'s errors; Q4, option chosen: "Red counter until opened (Rec.)"): **Errors turn the counter red, with their number** (e.g. `>_ 12 · 2 errors`), from the first error until the console is next opened; error lines fade like any other, in the error colour. Rejected: error lines that don't fade until the console is opened (they keep covering the showcase); error lines shown for 8 s.
- _`D428`_ (`live`, refined by _`D435`_; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); Q5, option chosen: "After load + 100ms (Rec.)"): **The console picks its mode once the page has loaded and 100 ms have passed**: lines logged earlier wait, then show alone if nothing rendered by then, or count in the counter otherwise; content appearing later still switches it from alone to hidden, for good. So a showcase logging before it renders, or React's first commit just after load, doesn't show the console alone for a moment. Rejected: showing the console on its first line (flickers); picking at `load` with no grace (React's first commit can follow it).
- _`D429`_ (`live`, refined by _`D436`_; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); Q6, option chosen: "Header bar + close, Esc (Rec.)"): **The open console has a slim header bar**, `Console · <count> lines` and a close button, and Escape closes it too (while focus is in the showcase frame). Rejected: the counter staying over the open console as its toggle (over its last line).
- _`D430`_ (`live`, changed by _`D433`_; refines _`D377`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md)): **What counts as rendered**: any element, or any text node with non-whitespace text, in `<body>`, except the showcase frame root itself, the console panel's element, and `script`, `style`, `link`, `meta`, `template` and `noscript` elements (the page's own scripts, and a showcase's injected styles); an element inside the showcase frame root counts, whatever it holds (an empty `<canvas>` renders).
- _`D431`_ (`live`, refined by _`D436`_; refines _`D369`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md)): **The open console grows a shorter showcase frame through `<body>`'s `min-height`**, set to the open panel's height while it is open and removed on closing, with `CONSOLE_GROWTH_EVENT` announcing the growth, so the frame script's guard never counts it; the panel itself is `position: fixed` in hidden and open modes, so it never adds to the content's height. The frame script is unchanged.
- _`D432`_ (`live`; refines _`D377`_ (SPEC-S10-presentation.md); review 2026-11-14 #3, QA, option chosen: "Once per load (Rec.)", after the user pointed out that "first error since close" would reopen it after every close): **An uncaught error or rejected promise opens the console by itself at most once per showcase frame load**: the first after the showcase rendered (or, when the mode is picked hidden, one before the pick); after that, errors only turn the counter red, until the showcase frame reloads. Rejected: every error reopening it (a repeating error can't be dismissed); never opening by itself (changes _`D377`_; a crash that empties the page leaves only a red counter); the first error since the last close (the agent's first proposal: reopens after every close).
- _`D433`_ (`live`; changes _`D430`_; review 2026-11-14 #7, QB, option chosen: "Text or visual only (Rec.)"): **Outside the showcase frame root, an element counts as rendered only if it holds non-whitespace text or is a visual element** (`img`, `picture`, `canvas`, `svg`, `video`, `audio`, `iframe`, `embed`, `object`, `input`, `button`, `select`, `textarea`), so a library's empty portal or toast container doesn't hide the console; inside the showcase frame root, any element counts, as _`D430`_ says. Rejected: any element counting (_`D430`_ as drafted).
- _`D434`_ (`live`; review 2026-11-14 #8, QC, option chosen: "Accept (Rec.)"): **In a showcase frame taller than the screen, the console's parts sit at its bottom, possibly out of view**: the fading lines, the counter and the open console are fixed to the showcase frame's bottom, which follows its content. Rejected: keeping them in view (the showroom telling the showcase frame its visible part on scroll; changes the frame script's contract).
- _`D435`_ (`live`; refines _`D428`_; review 2026-11-14 #5, QD, option chosen: "2 s ceiling (Rec.)"): **The mode is picked 2 s after `DOMContentLoaded` at the latest** (`CONSOLE_MODE_CEILING_MS`), so a page whose `load` event never fires (a stalled image, font or iframe) still shows its console. Rejected: 5 s; no ceiling.

- _`D436`_ (`live`; refines _`D424`_, _`D429`_, _`D431`_; at _`P10.5`_'s browser check, the user: "Even when fully expanded, the console should not grow over the line where the host element keeps the copy/source buttons", option chosen: "Always leave 32px (Rec.)"): **The open console panel always leaves the top 32 px of the showcase frame uncovered** (`CONSOLE_TOP_ROOM`, the code pane's header row), so it never covers the code pane's cycling button floating over the showcase frame's top-right corner (_`D407`_ (PLAN-P10.4-code-pane.md)): at most `100vh` less 32 px high, its log shrinking in a shorter showcase frame; opening it over shorter content grows `<body>` to the panel's height plus 32 px. In every `ts` and `react` showcase frame, in a block or inline. Rejected: leaving the room in blocks only (the showroom telling the showcase frame; a new message in the frame script's contract).
- _`D437`_ (`live`; refines _`D403`_, _`D407`_ (PLAN-P10.4-code-pane.md); at _`P10.5`_'s browser check, the user: "Min height of any showcase should be at least such that the console button doesn't overlap with the top buttons", option chosen: "Blocks, 64px, showroom (Rec.)"): **A showcase frame in a block is at least 64 px high** (`SHOWCASE_BLOCK_FRAME_MIN_HEIGHT`, `src/app/code-pane/`, through `ShowcaseFrame`'s new `minHeight`), the cycling button's row and a console panel's counter's row, so the two never overlap, whatever the showcase adapter; a linked showcase shown inline (no cycling button) keeps its own height. Brings the showroom into _`P10.5`_ (its goals left it out). Rejected: the console script growing `<body>` to 64 px once its counter shows (`ts` and `react` only); a minimum for every showcase frame, inline ones too.

- _`D438`_ (`live`; resolves _`L14`_ (PLAN.md); a fast-path change after _`P10.5`_, the user: "Lets limit max log lines and start dropping them after say 128. Every line dropped is counted and if dropped lines in their place is a top line stating how many lines have been dropped", option chosen: "Yes, as described (Rec.)"): **The console panel keeps the newest 128 lines** (`CONSOLE_MAX_LINES`): each line beyond them drops the oldest, and the panel's first line, styled as a `debug` line, says how many were dropped ("… 3 earlier lines dropped"); the counter and the header bar still count every line logged, dropped ones included; the fading lines are unchanged. Rejected: counting only the lines kept.
