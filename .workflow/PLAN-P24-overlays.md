# _`P24`_ Overlays in showcase frames

approved 2026-11-23 (option chosen: "Approve and commit (Rec.)")

From blox's brief (`../blox/.workflow/.temp/showroom-overlays/BRIEF.md`, blox's _`P4.2`_, _`O20`_, _`D131`_): the frame script measures only `<body>` and its children (_`D152`_, _`D168`_ (PLAN-P4.2-showcase-frames.md)), so content out of the normal flow never grows a showcase frame, and the showroom's box around the iframe clips it: an absolutely positioned dropdown, the content of a fixed layer (Angular CDK's overlay container, MUI's modal root) and a portal (Fluent UI's) are cut off at the frame's bottom. Opening one resizes neither `<body>` nor its children, so nothing re-measures either. One segment, with no child segments and no specification segment of its own (it changes _`P4.2`_'s decisions, not what the specification asks: a showcase frame as high as its content). Its choices were settled 2026-11-23 (Q1–Q4, all as recommended); no zero-context review (Q4).

## Goals

- While an overlay is open, a showcase frame is as high as the lowest bottom of what's rendered, overlays included; when it closes, the frame shrinks back to its content.
- The frame never grows for what the page itself clips or hides, nor follows its own height (`MAX_REPEATED_GROWTHS` stays).

## Decisions

- _`D894`_ (`live`, refined by _`D895`_, _`D896`_; changes _`D152`_, _`D168`_ (PLAN-P4.2-showcase-frames.md)): **The frame script also measures absolutely positioned content**: besides _`D168`_'s `<body>` and its children, the measured height takes the bottom (`getBoundingClientRect()`, which accounts for `transform`) of every element under `<body>` whose computed `position` is `absolute`, unless it is hidden (`display: none`, `visibility: hidden`) or clipped (_`D895`_), or shows nothing (_`D896`_). A `position: fixed` element's own box is never measured: it is placed against the frame's viewport, so it never needs more room, and a full-viewport layer left in place, empty, after its overlay closes (CDK's container) would otherwise keep the frame grown; its absolutely positioned content is measured by the rule above. The guard on repeated growth is unchanged: an overlay that follows the window grows with the frame until it stops.
- _`D895`_ (`live`; refines _`D894`_; Q1, option chosen: "Containing block (Rec.)"): **An element is clipped where the browser clips it**: from its containing block (its nearest ancestor whose `position` isn't `static`, or whose `transform` or `filter` isn't `none`, or whose `contain` holds `paint`, `layout`, `strict` or `content`) upward, stopping before `<body>`, an ancestor whose `overflow-y` isn't `visible` clips it; at an absolutely positioned ancestor the walk goes on from that ancestor's own containing block, and at a fixed one it stops (nothing above clips it). `<body>`'s own `overflow` is the viewport's, never counted. Rejected: the brief's rule, any ancestor below `<body>` that clips (it would skip an overlay escaping a clipping box, e.g. a dropdown in a scrolling card positioned against something outside it).
- _`D896`_ (`live`; refines _`D894`_; Q2, option chosen: "Skip both (Rec.)"): **What shows nothing isn't measured**: an element whose computed `clip` isn't `auto` (the visually hidden pattern of Fluent UI, MUI, Angular CDK's live announcer and Tailwind's `sr-only`, 1×1 px, often at the end of `<body>`), and a box with no width or no height (Fluent UI's portal node). Rejected: only `display` and `visibility` (a frame a pixel or two higher than its content).
- _`D897`_ (`live`; Q3, option chosen: "Re-measure on end (Rec.)"): **Animated overlays are measured once they settle**: `transitionend` and `animationend`, listened to on the document in the capture phase, re-measure (an overlay opening with a scale transform, MUI's `Popover` or Angular Material's menus, is measured at its first frame, scaled down, when it is added); and each measured absolutely positioned element is observed by the `ResizeObserver`, as `<body>`'s children are, so a dropdown whose content loads later re-measures. Rejected: the brief's observers only (an animated overlay as high as its first frame).
- _`D898`_ (`live`): **Re-measuring is batched to one measure per animation frame**: the `ResizeObserver`, a `MutationObserver` on `<body>` (`childList`, `attributes`, `subtree`; overlays open by adding nodes, or by changing classes and styles), `transitionend`, `animationend` and `load` each ask for a measure, run once at the next animation frame (`requestAnimationFrame`, or a `setTimeout` of 0 where it is missing); the first measure, when the frame script starts, stays immediate. Walking every element's computed style is the costly part, and batching keeps it to one walk per frame with changes.
- _`D899`_ (`live`): **Documentation and tests**:
  - `src/frame/index.test.ts`: the stubbed `window` gets elements under `<body>` (each with its parent, computed style and rect), a `MutationObserver` and `requestAnimationFrame` driven by hand, and document listeners; a test each for the brief's checks (a 300 px panel below a 40 px trigger; the panel removed and set to `display: none`; a fixed full-viewport layer left in place, empty; a panel inside an `overflow: auto` box), a clipping ancestor below the containing block (_`D895`_), `visibility: hidden`, `clip` and an empty portal (_`D896`_), the end of a transition (_`D897`_), several changes in one animation frame measured once (_`D898`_), and the guard stopping an overlay anchored to the viewport's bottom; the existing height tests run their batched measures; the `describe`'s purpose comment;
  - `README.md` (the measured height, next to "Don't size a showcase to its viewport"; overlays that keep themselves within the window, e.g. MUI `Popover`'s `max-height`, never grow the frame, so a showcase lifts that limit in its theme), `CHANGELOG.md` (`## Unreleased` › Changed), `AGENTS.md` (the growth guard's gotcha, the frame script's tests in Testing); the Glossary's "Frame script" still holds;
  - the user checks the brief's checks, and an animated overlay, in a browser, on a scratch host repository served at the checkpoint.
