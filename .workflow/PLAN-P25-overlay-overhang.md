# _`P25`_ Overlays over the page

approved 2026-11-24 (option chosen: "Approve and commit (Rec.)"), after its zero-context review

_`S19`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md): a showcase's open overlay is drawn over the page around its showcase frame, below or above it, without moving the page, and a press outside it reaches the showcase. One segment, with no child segments. It builds on _`P24`_'s measuring (PLAN-P24-overlays.md).

## Decisions

- _`D905`_ (`live`, changed by _`D918`_; changes _`D894`_, _`D896`_ (PLAN-P24-overlays.md)): **A box filling the frame's viewport is a layer, not an overlay**: an absolutely positioned element whose box covers the whole viewport (an Angular Material or CDK backdrop, `position: absolute; inset: 0` in a fixed layer) is neither measured nor an overlay. Measured, it would hold the showcase frame at its height of the moment while open, and, clipped to the overlays (_`D908`_), it would take the presses on the whole showcase frame's overhang.
  - Evidence: spike 2026-11-23 (`./.workflow/.temp/spike-overlay-overhang/`, a Material-like menu with a transparent backdrop): with the backdrop skipped, only the menu covered the page, and a press on the page below closed it through the relay (_`D909`_).
- _`D906`_ (`live`, refined by _`D915`_, changed by _`D919`_; refines _`D894`_ (PLAN-P24-overlays.md)): **The frame script reports its content and its overlays apart**: every `resize` message carries `offset` (how far the page is pushed down, _`D907`_), `flow` (the bottom of its in-flow content, _`D168`_ (PLAN-P4.2-showcase-frames.md)), `height` (the bottom of everything, overlays included, as now), `boxes` (each overlay's box reaching out of `offset`…`flow`, in the page's pixels) and `open` (whether any overlay shows). With no overlay out, `offset` is 0 and `flow` is `height`, as before. The growth guard watches `height`, as now.
- _`D907`_ (`live`, refined by _`D915`_, changed by _`D926`_): **Overlays above the top push the page down**: while an overlay reaches above the page's top, the frame script adds the amount to `<html>`'s top padding (on top of the page's own, read when the push starts), and measures again at the next animation frame. The push only grows while an overlay is open (by what still reaches above), and is reset once none is: an overlay placed above its trigger moves down with the page, fits, and would otherwise be unpushed and reach above again, forever.
  - Evidence: spike 2026-11-23: a CSS-placed panel (`bottom: 100%`) and a pane forced above its trigger, re-placed every animation frame (as floating-ui's `autoUpdate` with a fixed `top` placement): the user, "Both cases in the spike that open up work perfectly!".
- _`D908`_ (`live`, refined by _`D915`_, _`D920`_, _`D922`_, _`D924`_, _`D927`_): **The showroom pulls the page back and clips the showcase frame to its overlays**: the iframe is `height` high, with a margin of `-offset` at its top and `-(height - flow)` at its bottom, so it takes `flow - offset` of the page; its `clip-path` is a `path()` of its content's rectangle (`offset`…`flow`, the full width) and each box, as clockwise rectangles whose union the nonzero rule fills (`getOverhangClipPath()`, a pure function). `clip-path` clips both painting and hit-testing: an opaque page background (MUI's `CssBaseline`) never covers the page, and the page beside an overlay stays clickable and selectable. A showcase frame's minimum height (a block's `demo` view) counts as content. While an overlay is out:
  - the showcase frame's box lets it out vertically, still clipping sideways (`overflow-x: clip; overflow-y: visible`);
  - the box, and the showcase block holding it (a CSS container, so a stacking context of its own), are raised over what follows them (`position: relative; z-index: 1`), below the showroom's header and drawers;
  - the console panel stays anchored to the content (it sits in the box, which keeps the content's height), and a block's "Not built" placeholder gets the content's height;
  - a hidden showcase frame (a `console` view with no `demo`, _`D570`_ (PLAN-P15.4-views.md)) never lets an overlay out, nor receives presses.
  - Evidence: spike 2026-11-23 (a harness copying the block's structure: a grid, the box clipping, a console line at its bottom, a code pane beside, the showcase sidebar's scroll container, both themes): the user, "Everything looks great"; checked in Chrome/Edge and Firefox, not Safari (_`K32`_ (SPEC-pitfalls.md)).
- _`D909`_ (`live`, refined by _`D915`_, _`D921`_): **Presses outside are relayed**: the showroom listens to real `pointerdown`s on its own document (in the capture phase), and the frame script reports its own real ones (a `pointer` message); each is posted, as an `outside-pointer` message in the receiving showcase frame's own coordinates, to every other showcase frame reporting `open`. The frame script replays `pointerdown`, `mousedown`, `pointerup`, `mouseup` and `click` on a safe target: the topmost element at the press's point, or else at the viewport's corners and edges' middles, that is in no overlay and is `<body>`, `<html>` or inside a fixed layer (a backdrop), else `<body>`; always at a point inside the viewport (MUI's `ClickAwayListener` takes a press outside it for one on the scrollbar). It never replays on the page's own content.
  - Evidence: spike 2026-11-23: overlays closing on `click` (Fluent UI, MUI), on `pointerdown` (floating-ui, CDK) and on their backdrop's click (Angular Material) all closed on a press on the page, and on one in another showcase frame: the user, "Works great! Even clicking in a different iframe closes a menu/dialog in the current iframe!".
- _`D910`_ (`live`, refined by _`D917`_, _`D928`_): **Documentation and tests**:
  - `src/frame/`: the messages' validation (`resize`'s new fields, `pointer`, `outside-pointer`), the measure's `offset`, `flow`, `boxes` and `open` (a backdrop skipped, _`D905`_), the push and its guard (_`D907`_), the `pointer` report, and the replay's target and events on the stubbed `window` (`elementFromPoint` and `dispatchEvent` stubbed);
  - `src/app/showcases/`: `getOverhangClipPath()`, the margins and styles of a showcase frame rendered on the server with a given starting overhang (as no message comes there), and the relay's recipients as a pure function of the frames' reports; the block's raising in `src/app/code-pane/`;
  - `README.md` (overlays shown over the page; below and above only; a press outside closes them), `CHANGELOG.md`, `AGENTS.md` (Testing, and a gotcha on the overhang: the clip, the stacking, the push's guard), the Glossary (the terms the draft's dialog settles);
  - the user checks a scratch host repository with the spike's showcases as `html` showcases, served at the checkpoint, in blocks, beside the code pane in each of its states, with the console panel, in the showcase sidebar, and in both themes.

### The opt-in, and the Glossary

- _`D915`_ (`live`, refined by _`D923`_; refines _`D906`_–_`D909`_; for _`D911`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md)): **The opt-in reaches the frame script through its URL**: the scan reads the `overlays` key (`src/markdown/`), `check` reports its misuse (_`D912`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md)), and the host information's `Showcase` gets `overlays: true` when it is given (left out otherwise; the exported type, so `README.md`'s Usage), carried in the shown host information. The showroom adds `overlays=1` to such a showcase frame's URL beside `theme` (`getThemedFrameUrl()`), so nothing is rebuilt; the frame script reads it when it starts. Without it, the frame script measures and posts as _`P24`_ made it (only `height` beyond the push-free measure, no push, no `boxes`, never `open`) and replays nothing, but still reports its presses (_`D914`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md)); the showroom only lets an opted-in showcase frame's overlays out, and only relays presses to opted-in ones.
- _`D916`_ (`live`; Q3, option chosen: "Add both (Rec.)"): **Two Glossary terms**: "Overlay": a showcase's content placed outside its page's flow (absolutely positioned, and showing), such as a dropdown, a menu, a popover or a dialog's pane, which, for a showcase with the `overlays` key, may be drawn over the page around its showcase frame; "Overhang": the parts of a showcase frame above or below its content, where only its overlays show, drawn over the page without moving it. Code and documentation use them.
- _`D917`_ (`live`, refined by _`D928`_; refines _`D910`_): **The opt-in's tests and documentation**: the key in `src/markdown/` (a bare word; repeated, or with a value, an error), `check`'s errors on a fixture (`showcase-overlays`: the key on a code block showcase, a linked showcase shown as a block and one inline; misused on a virtual file with no showcase adapter, with `view=source`, and on a showcase whose adapter's default views have no `demo`), `Showcase.overlays` in `src/vite/`'s shown host information and the URL in `src/app/showcases/`; `README.md`'s marker keys and its syntax cheat-sheet, and a live example in its Showcases part (a short `html` showcase with a dropdown, with `overlays`), `AGENTS.md`'s Exports and Testing, `CHANGELOG.md`.

### The review's outcomes

From the zero-context review of 2026-11-23 (`./.workflow/.temp/review-p25/REVIEW.md`; one reviewer; fixes and skips as listed there, questions as chosen below).

- _`D918`_ (`live`; changes _`D905`_; review 2026-11-23 #1): **A layer is a box filling the viewport inside a fixed layer, never one filling it alone**: an absolutely positioned element counts as a layer (neither measured nor an overlay) only when it fills the viewport and its containing-block walk reaches a `position: fixed` ancestor (a backdrop). Judged by size alone, it would feed back on the frame's own height (the viewport is the iframe): a showcase laid out absolutely, with nothing in flow, would shrink to its minimum height for good, opted in or not. A test covers that case.
- _`D919`_ (`live`; changes _`D906`_; review 2026-11-23 #7, #18): **`resize` is posted whenever any of its fields changes**, not only `height`: an overlay opening inside the content changes only `open`, one moving sideways only `boxes`. The growth guard holds only `height` (at its last reported value once stopped), and the other fields are still posted. Its new fields are optional in `isFrameMessage()`, and a showcase frame that didn't opt in sends none of them.
- _`D920`_ (`live`; refines _`D908`_; review 2026-11-23 #2, #4, #5, #16; #2's question, decided: only while raised, as the user's own check showed no visible leak and a child's background can only show at most the 4 px radius): **The showroom's layout while an overlay is out**: the block's grid (its border and rounded corners, `overflow: hidden`) is `overflow: visible` only while it is raised, as the showcase frame's box is; the box is always `display: flow-root`, so the iframe's negative margins never collapse through it (moving the console panel into the overhang); in the showcase sidebar, the sticky header bar stays above a raised box (_`D927`_'s levels); the console panel's counter and lines paint over an overlay crossing the content's bottom, which is accepted.
- _`D921`_ (`live`; refines _`D909`_; review 2026-11-23 #6, #12, #13, #17, #18; #12, option chosen: "Split + accept focus (Rec.)"): **The relay's details**:
  - only real presses (`isTrusted`) are reported by the frame script and relayed by the showroom, so a replayed press never bounces between showcase frames;
  - `pointerdown` and `mousedown` are replayed at the real `pointerdown`, and `pointerup`, `mouseup` and `click` only at the real `click` (an `outside-pointer` message with its phase), so a touch that scrolls the page (cancelled, no `click`) closes only overlays that close on `pointerdown`, as natively; an overlay library putting focus back on its trigger, in the showcase frame, is accepted, and checked by hand;
  - the showroom keeps a module-level registry in `src/app/showcases/` of every mounted showcase frame (on the page and in the showcase sidebar): its window, its opt-in, `open`, and when it opened; an entry is removed on unmount and reset on `start`, and `pointer` is accepted only from a registered window;
  - the frame script uses `window.PointerEvent`, `window.MouseEvent` and `window.document.elementFromPoint` (never bare globals), replays mouse events only where `PointerEvent` is missing, accepts `outside-pointer` only from its parent, and `pointer` and `outside-pointer` carry finite coordinates, validated.
- _`D922`_ (`live`; refines _`D908`_; review 2026-11-23 #8): **The clip's content rectangle has a constant, very large width**, as the box clips sideways: `getOverhangClipPath()` needs no width, and the clip never goes stale when the showcase frame's width changes (the code pane's states, the container query, the window).
- _`D923`_ (`live`; refines _`D915`_; review 2026-11-23 #11): **The showcase frame's URL follows `overlays` too**: `ShowcaseFrame` recomputes its URL when the showcase's `overlays` changes, as when its URL does, so adding or removing the key in `serve` reloads it opted in or out; `getShownChange()` (`src/cli/serve/`) and `applyShowcaseUpdates()` (`src/app/host/`) are tested for the key.
- _`D924`_ (`live`; refines _`D908`_, _`D913`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md); review 2026-11-23 #9, option chosen: "Neither (Rec.)"): **A showcase frame whose views aren't shown is never let out, and receives no presses**: hidden (_`D570`_ (PLAN-P15.4-views.md)), or collapsed in the showcase sidebar (`fill`); it grows as _`P24`_ made it.
- _`D926`_ (`live`; changes _`D907`_; review 2026-11-23 #14, option chosen for #14a: "When its causes go (Rec.)"): **The push is reset once the overlays that caused it are gone**: the frame script remembers the overlay elements that reached above when the push grew, and resets it once none of them shows, so a permanent decoration never holds it. The push adds to `<html>`'s computed top padding. `README.md` says that fixed elements at a showcase frame's edges are clipped while an overlay is out, and that overlays positioned `fixed` (floating-ui's `strategy: 'fixed'`) are never measured.
- _`D927`_ (`live`; refines _`D908`_; review 2026-11-23 #15, option chosen: "Latest opened on top (Rec.)"): **The block whose overlay opened last is on top**: a raised block is at level 1, and the one whose showcase frame reported `open` most recently at level 2; the showcase sidebar's sticky header bar is at 3.
- _`D928`_ (`live`; refines _`D910`_, _`D917`_; review 2026-11-23 #19, #20): **More documentation and tests**: `AGENTS.md`'s gotchas ("The frame script stops reporting growth", "A console view with no demo view runs its showcase frame hidden", "A container query's container needs its width given", and a new one on overlays over the page), its Layout line of `src/frame/`, and its Testing (the code pane's raised block, the placeholder's height from `flow - offset`); `ShowcaseFrame`'s starting overhang for the server-side rendering (`initialOverhang`, as `initialConsole`); the live `README.md` showcase lists `include=markdown` first and names its marker as written (_`D606`_, _`D607`_ (PLAN-P15.6-included-keywords.md)), and `src/cli/check/index.integration.test.ts` checks it; marker examples of both kinds (a fence's info string, `html showcase overlays`; a link's title, `"showcase=react block overlays"`).

Skipped: #3 (the drawers are portalled at `z-index: 1000000`, above any raised block; the header isn't sticky, so an overlay over it is as over any text, _`D901`_ (SPEC-S19-overlays.md)), #21 (folded into _`D921`_'s registry).
